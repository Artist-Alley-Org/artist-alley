---
title: Design pillars
description: The three principles we use to make product decisions.
---

Artist Alley is built around three pillars. When a design decision is
ambiguous, we resolve it by asking which pillar the choice serves best.

## 1. Artist-first, not archive-first

Artists upload and forget. Metadata happens automatically where possible.

- File-format defaults: dropping a folder Just Works.
- EXIF, dimensions, color profile, embedded thumbnails parsed on ingest.
- Optional automatic tags via embeddings (pgvector).
- Required fields are kept to an absolute minimum.

**UX target:** [ArtStation](https://www.artstation.com/) levels of
simplicity for upload.

## 2. Review mode

Reviewers come back to Artist Alley over and over. The tool is shaped
for that loop.

- Tracks unreviewed assets since your last session.
- Remembers your spot — you can leave mid-batch and resume.
- Async commenting (Frame.io-style on top of each asset).
- Live presenter sessions over SSE for sync review meetings.

## 3. Three-click rule

Any common action — upload, find, comment, share, approve — reachable in
three clicks or fewer. Friction is the enemy of an archive that gets used.

When a workflow takes more than three clicks, the design is wrong, not
the user.
