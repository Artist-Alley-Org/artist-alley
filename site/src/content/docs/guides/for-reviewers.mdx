The principles guiding the reviewer experience are described in the
[design pillars](/whitepaper/pillars/#2-review-mode).

What we're aiming for:

- A "review queue" of unreviewed assets since your last session.
- Persistent spot-marking so you can come back to where you left off.
- Async comments anchored to a region of the asset (Frame.io-style).
- Live presenter sessions over SSE for synchronous review meetings.
